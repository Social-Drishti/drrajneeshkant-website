Sciatica Treatment

Non-Surgical Treatment for Sciatica Pain

Sciatica is a condition characterised by pain that radiates from the lower back through the buttock and down the back of the leg. It occurs when the sciatic nerve, the longest nerve in the body, becomes compressed or irritated. The pain can range from a mild ache to a sharp, burning sensation, and is often accompanied by numbness, tingling, or weakness in the affected leg.

Non-surgical chiropractic treatment can effectively relieve sciatic nerve compression by addressing the structural causes of the condition. Through spinal decompression therapy, targeted adjustments, and rehabilitation exercises, the pressure on the sciatic nerve is reduced, allowing the body to heal naturally.

Common Causes of Sciatica

Herniated Disc: A herniated or slipped disc in the lower spine is one of the most common causes of sciatica. The displaced disc material presses on the sciatic nerve root, producing radiating pain.

Spinal Stenosis: Narrowing of the spinal canal can compress the sciatic nerve, particularly in older adults. This condition often develops gradually over time.

Piriformis Syndrome: The piriformis muscle, located deep in the buttock, can spasm or tighten and compress the sciatic nerve that runs nearby, mimicking sciatica symptoms.

Degenerative Disc Disease: Gradual wear and tear of the spinal discs can lead to instability and nerve irritation in the lumbar spine.

Benefits of Sciatica Treatment

Nerve Pain Relief: Spinal decompression and targeted adjustments reduce pressure on the sciatic nerve, providing significant pain relief.

Improved Mobility: Treatment restores normal movement patterns and reduces the protective muscle guarding that limits activity.

Reduced Numbness: By addressing nerve compression, treatment can reduce or eliminate the numbness and tingling that often accompanies sciatica.

Core Strengthening: Rehabilitation exercises strengthen the core and spinal stabilisers, providing better support for the lumbar spine.
