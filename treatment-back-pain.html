Back Pain Treatment

Effective Back Pain Treatment Without Surgery

Back pain is one of the most common health concerns affecting millions of people worldwide. Whether it stems from poor posture, long working hours, muscle strain, a sedentary lifestyle, or underlying spinal conditions, back pain can significantly limit your daily activities and overall quality of life.

At Back To Nature Spine Clinic, Dr. Rajneesh Kant provides evidence-informed chiropractic and physiotherapy care designed to address the root cause of your back pain rather than simply masking the symptoms. Through precise spinal assessment, targeted manual therapy, and personalised rehabilitation exercises, the goal is to restore function, reduce pain, and help you return to the activities you enjoy.

Common Causes of Back Pain

Understanding the underlying cause of your back pain is essential for effective treatment. Some of the most frequent causes we encounter include:

Poor Posture: Prolonged sitting, slouching, and incorrect workstation ergonomics place excessive stress on the spinal structures, leading to muscular imbalances and pain over time.

Muscle Strain: Overstretching or tearing of the back muscles due to heavy lifting, sudden movements, or repetitive stress is one of the most common acute causes of back pain.

Herniated Disc: When the soft inner material of a spinal disc pushes through its outer layer, it can irritate nearby nerves and cause significant pain, numbness, or weakness.

Sciatica: Compression or irritation of the sciatic nerve can cause pain that radiates from the lower back through the buttock and down the leg.

Age-Related Degeneration: Natural wear and tear of spinal discs, joints, and ligaments over time can lead to conditions such as degenerative disc disease and spinal stenosis.

Benefits of Our Back Pain Treatment

Our treatment approach focuses on delivering lasting outcomes through conservative, non-invasive methods:

Non-Surgical Treatment: Chiropractic care and physiotherapy offer effective relief without the risks and recovery time associated with surgical procedures.

Improved Mobility: Targeted mobilisation and exercise therapy help restore full range of motion to the spine and surrounding joints.

Pain Relief: Spinal adjustments, soft tissue therapy, and modalities work together to reduce pain intensity and frequency.

Posture Correction: We address the postural habits and imbalances that contribute to recurring back pain.

Core Strengthening: A strong core provides essential support to the spine, reducing the risk of future episodes.
