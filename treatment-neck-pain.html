Cervical & Neck Pain Treatment

Targeted Treatment for Cervical Pain and Neck Stiffness

Cervical pain and neck stiffness have become increasingly common in today's world of desk work and constant device usage. Hours spent hunched over computers and phones place sustained stress on the cervical spine, leading to pain, stiffness, headaches, and reduced range of motion.

Chiropractic treatment addresses the root cause of cervical discomfort rather than simply suppressing symptoms. Through precise assessment of the cervical vertebrae, targeted adjustments, and rehabilitation exercises, Dr. Rajneesh Kant helps patients restore proper neck function and reduce dependency on pain medication.

Common Causes of Cervical Pain

Poor Posture: Forward head posture and slouched sitting positions place excessive strain on the neck muscles and cervical joints, leading to chronic discomfort over time.

Tech Neck: The repetitive habit of looking down at smartphones and tablets puts significant pressure on the cervical spine, often resulting in stiffness and pain.

Cervical Spondylosis: Age-related wear and tear of the cervical discs and joints can cause chronic neck pain, stiffness, and in some cases, nerve compression.

Muscle Strain: Overuse, sleeping in an awkward position, or sudden movements can strain the neck muscles, leading to acute pain and restricted movement.

Disc Problems: Herniated or bulging cervical discs can compress nearby nerve roots, causing pain that may radiate into the shoulders, arms, or hands.

Benefits of Cervical Pain Treatment

Pain Relief: Gentle chiropractic adjustments and soft tissue techniques reduce cervical pain and muscle tension without medication.

Improved Range of Motion: Targeted mobilisation restores the neck's natural range of movement, allowing you to turn and tilt your head comfortably.

Posture Correction: Treatment addresses the postural imbalances that cause and perpetuate cervical pain.

Headache Reduction: Many tension headaches and cervicogenic headaches originate from cervical dysfunction and respond well to chiropractic care.
